AI

The third paw at the bench: our own AI workshop assistant, built at home and sharper every day.

What our assistant is

Not a chatbot that only talks. Our assistant runs on a small home server and works with real things: files, notes, printers, gadgets on the desk. It remembers through a notebook of its own, so every day starts where the last one ended.

What it does

  • Talks on every device. Phone, laptop and little desk gadgets, by typing or by voice.
  • Watches the workshop. Keeps an eye on the 3D printers and the home computers, and sends a message when something needs a hand.
  • Designs with us. Turns a plain description into a printable part, works out what a print costs, and diagnoses failed prints.
  • Handles the paperwork. Reads letters, explains them plainly and keeps track of deadlines.
  • Improves itself. Reviews its own mistakes every night and fixes the rules that caused them.

Learning alongside

This page is part of that: built by our assistant, learned along the way. More projects will land here as we build them.
